One skill per stage — brainstorm, requirement, design, code, test, ship — each
handing a reviewed artifact to the next and stopping at a hard gate rather than
cascading. Nothing is assumed: uncertainty is surfaced, claims are cited, and
no stage begins until you approve the one before it. Early, and honest about
which stages exist.
